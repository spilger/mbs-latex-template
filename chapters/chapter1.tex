\chapter{Task}

Some websites offer ghostwritten wedding speeches. After answering a questionnaire and paying \$149, you will receive a custom toast that is professionally written within three business days. Other websites sell pre-written wedding speeches for \$19.95, which come with a money-back guarantee.

Suppose, on your wedding day, your best man/maid of honor delivers a heartwarming toast, a speech so moving it brings tears to your eyes. You later learn that it was bought online.

\begin{enumerate}
    \item Would you care?
    \item Would the toast mean less than it did at first, before you knew it was written by a paid professional?
    \item Finally, do you think that some market mechanisms change the value or meaning of the goods and social practices?
\end{enumerate}
